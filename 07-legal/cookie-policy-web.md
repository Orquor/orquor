# Cookie Policy — Orquor public website

**Version 2026-05** · Applies to **orquor.com** and subdomains.

---

This Cookie Policy explains the cookies that may be set when you visit Orquor websites. We try to use as few cookies as possible, and we never use cookies for advertising or cross-site behavioral tracking.

## What is a cookie

A cookie is a small text file stored by your browser on your device when you visit a website. Cookies allow a site to remember information about your visit between page loads or visits.

## Cookies we use

| Cookie | Purpose | Type | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `orquor_session` | Session cookie for authenticated areas (clinical.orquor.com, console.orquor.com) | Strictly necessary | Session (deleted on browser close) |
| `orquor_csrf` | Cross-site request forgery protection for forms | Strictly necessary | Session |
| `orquor_consent` | Records your cookie-banner choice so we do not show it again | Strictly necessary | 12 months |
| `plausible_ignore` | If you opt out of Plausible Analytics, this cookie remembers your choice locally | Strictly necessary | 12 months |

## Cookies we do not use

- **Google Analytics or any cross-site tracker.** We use Plausible Analytics, which does not use cookies, does not store IP addresses, does not track users across sites.
- **Advertising cookies.** No Meta Pixel, no Twitter/X conversion pixel, no LinkedIn Insight Tag, no Google Ads tracking.
- **Behavioral profiling cookies.** We do not build profiles of visitors.
- **Third-party social-media cookies.** When we embed external content like YouTube videos, we use the "no-cookie" variant where available.

## Your choices

- **Browser settings.** All modern browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. If you block strictly-necessary cookies, the authenticated areas of orquor.com may not work; the public site will work normally.
- **Plausible opt-out.** If you do not want even our anonymous analytics, you can disable JavaScript on orquor.com or set the `plausible_ignore=true` cookie in your browser developer tools. We respect Do Not Track browser headers.

## Third-party services

When you use services we link to from Orquor (such as YouTube videos, Calendly scheduling links, the Hostinger-hosted blog), those third parties may set their own cookies governed by their privacy policies. We have no control over third-party cookies.

## Changes

We update this Cookie Policy when our cookie use changes. Material changes are accompanied by a banner on orquor.com.

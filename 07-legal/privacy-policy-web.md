We comply with Peruvian Law 29733 on Protection of Personal Data, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) where applicable, and other applicable laws.

## 2. What we collect

When you visit orquor.com or any subdomain, we collect:

- **Site analytics** — page visits, referrer, country (not city), device class, anonymized via Plausible Analytics. No personal identifiers, no cookies for cross-site tracking.
- **Email submissions** — if you fill a contact form, subscribe to the newsletter, or request the whitepaper, we collect your email address and any fields you complete.
- **Application data** — if you apply to Orquor Academy or to join the team, we collect the materials you submit (resume, cover letter, contact information).

## 3. What we do not collect

- We do not use Google Analytics or similar tracking that builds cross-site profiles.
- We do not embed third-party advertising trackers (no Meta Pixel, no Twitter/X conversion pixel).
- We do not buy or sell personal data.
- We do not engage in behavioral profiling for advertising.

## 4. How we use what we collect

- **Site analytics** — to understand which content resonates and improve the public site. Aggregated and anonymous.
- **Email submissions** — to send you what you requested (whitepaper, newsletter, response to your inquiry). We only email you with content related to what you opted into.
- **Application data** — to evaluate your application. We retain applications for 12 months and then delete unless you ask us to keep them on file longer.

## 5. Legal basis (GDPR)

For EU residents:
- Consent under Article 6(1)(a) for marketing emails
- Legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) for site analytics and security
- Contract under Article 6(1)(b) for prospect and customer communications

## 6. Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer your personal data to third parties for their own purposes. We do use the following processors strictly to deliver services to you:

- **Hostinger** — web hosting and email infrastructure
- **Plausible Analytics** — privacy-respecting site analytics (self-hostable; we currently use their EU-based service)
- **Resend** — transactional email
- **Skool / Teachable** — Orquor Academy hosting (when you enroll)
- **Stripe / Lemon Squeezy** — payment processing (when you purchase Academy access)

Each of these is contractually bound to process data only on our instructions.

## 7. Cookies

We use a minimum of cookies. See the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy.html) for the complete list. We do not use cookies for advertising, cross-site tracking, or behavioral profiling.

## 8. Retention

- Marketing email subscribers: until you unsubscribe
- Application data: 12 months from submission, then deleted (unless you explicitly ask us to keep your application active)
- Site analytics: aggregated only, no individual records retained beyond 90 days
- Newsletter unsubscribes: retained as suppression list to prevent re-subscription via list import (legal requirement under CAN-SPAM and equivalent regulations)

## 9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

- **Access** your personal data
- **Rectify** inaccurate data
- **Erase** your data (the "right to be forgotten")
- **Restrict** processing
- **Portability** — receive your data in machine-readable format
- **Object** to processing where based on legitimate interest
- **Withdraw consent** at any time
- **Complain** to a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email **privacy@orquor.com** from a verifiable address. We respond within 20 business days, free of charge for reasonable requests.

You also have the right to file a complaint with:
- **Peru**: Authority for Personal Data Protection (ANPDP), MINJUS
- **EU**: Your member-state data protection authority
- **California**: California Attorney General
- **Brazil**: ANPD

## 10. International transfers

We operate from Peru. The processors listed in Section 6 are located in Europe, the United States, and Peru. International data transfers happen under:

- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission for EU-origin data
- Equivalent transfer mechanisms recognized under Peruvian Law 29733
- Brazilian LGPD's international transfer provisions for Brazilian residents

## 11. Security

We protect your personal data using technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

- TLS 1.2+ encryption for all data in transit
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- Multi-factor authentication for all employee access
- Quarterly security review

In the event of a breach involving your personal data, we will notify you within 72 hours of detection (and in any case within the timeframes required by applicable law).

## 12. Children

Our public website and Academy are not intended for individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, contact privacy@orquor.com and we will delete it.

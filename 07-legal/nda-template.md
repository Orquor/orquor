## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public, proprietary, or commercially sensitive information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party"), in any form — oral, written, electronic, visual, or otherwise — that is identified at disclosure as confidential, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential under the circumstances.

Confidential Information includes, without limitation:
- Technical specifications, source code, architectures, model weights, prompts, verifier suites
- Pricing, financial information, business plans, customer lists, sales pipelines
- Clinical workflows, hospital operational data, patient demographics (regardless of PHI status)
- Strategies, research, methodologies, evaluation benchmarks
- Information shared during pilot evaluation or pre-contract discovery

Confidential Information does **not** include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:
(a) was already in its possession without obligation of confidentiality at the time of disclosure;
(b) was or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement;
(c) was independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information;
(d) is rightfully received from a third party without obligation of confidentiality;
(e) is required to be disclosed by law, court order, or regulatory authority (with prompt notice to the Disclosing Party, where legally permissible, to allow protective measures).

## 2. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party shall:

2.1 Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating, or performing the proposed business relationship between the Parties (the "Purpose").

2.2 Hold Confidential Information in strict confidence and protect it with the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of similar importance, but in no event less than reasonable care.

2.3 Limit disclosure of Confidential Information within its own organization to those employees, contractors, advisors, and affiliates who have a need to know for the Purpose, who are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement.

2.4 Not reproduce, copy, summarize, or extract from Confidential Information except as necessary for the Purpose, and clearly mark any such reproductions as confidential.

2.5 Not use Confidential Information for any purpose competitive with the Disclosing Party, including but not limited to product development, sales targeting, or recruiting of personnel.

## 3. Term

This Agreement is effective on the date above and remains in effect for two (2) years, or until terminated by mutual written agreement, whichever comes first. The confidentiality obligations in Section 2 survive termination for an additional three (3) years (five (5) years for trade-secret-grade information).

## 4. Return or Destruction

Upon written request of the Disclosing Party, or at termination of the Purpose, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession, certify destruction in writing if requested, and retain no copies, except as required by archival, legal, or regulatory obligation, in which case continuing confidentiality applies indefinitely.

## 5. No License

No license, ownership, intellectual property right, or other interest in the Confidential Information is granted under this Agreement, whether by implication, estoppel, or otherwise. All Confidential Information remains the sole property of the Disclosing Party.

## 6. No Obligation to Transact

Neither this Agreement nor the disclosure or receipt of Confidential Information creates an obligation on either Party to enter into any further business relationship.

## 7. Equitable Relief

The Parties acknowledge that breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm not adequately compensable by monetary damages, and that the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief or other equitable remedies in addition to any other remedies available, without the need to post bond.

## 8. Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Peru (or, if both Parties are non-Peruvian, the State of Delaware, USA). Disputes shall be resolved exclusively in the competent courts of Lima, Peru (or New Castle County, Delaware, as applicable).

## 9. Miscellaneous

9.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes prior oral or written communications.

9.2 No amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

9.3 If any provision is held invalid, the remainder remains in effect.

9.4 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts (including electronic signatures), each of which is deemed an original and which together constitute one agreement.

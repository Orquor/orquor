This Contractor Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into between:

**Orquor S.A.C.** ("Company") and

**[Contractor Legal Name]** ("Contractor")

Effective Date: [DATE]

## 1. Services

Contractor shall provide the services described in **Exhibit A — Statement of Work** ("SoW"), as may be updated by mutual written agreement.

## 2. Compensation

2.1 Company shall pay Contractor the fees set forth in the SoW.

2.2 Invoicing occurs monthly. Payment is made within fifteen (15) business days of invoice receipt, by bank transfer or Wise / Payoneer, to the account designated by Contractor.

2.3 Taxes: Contractor is solely responsible for all taxes, social contributions, and other obligations of any jurisdiction in which Contractor resides or operates.

## 3. Independent Contractor Status

3.1 Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, joint-venturer, or partner of Company. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship.

3.2 Contractor controls the manner and means of performing the Services, subject to satisfaction of deliverables described in the SoW.

3.3 Contractor is not entitled to employee benefits, including paid leave, health insurance, retirement contributions, or severance.

## 4. Intellectual Property

All Work Product created by Contractor in connection with the Services is assigned to Company per the **IP Assignment Agreement** executed concurrently herewith. The IP Assignment Agreement is incorporated by reference and is a material part of this Agreement.

## 5. Confidentiality

The Mutual NDA executed between the Parties applies to all Confidential Information exchanged under this Agreement. The NDA's confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement.

## 6. Non-Solicitation

For twelve (12) months following termination of this Agreement, Contractor shall not solicit Company's employees, contractors, customers, or active prospects (defined as those in Company's sales pipeline at the time of termination) for any business purpose competitive with Company.

## 7. Term and Termination

7.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated under this Section 7 or by completion of the SoW.

7.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on fifteen (15) days written notice.

7.3 **Termination for Cause.** Company may terminate immediately for material breach, including breach of NDA, breach of IP Assignment, or violation of applicable law.

7.4 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, Contractor shall (a) cease performance of Services, (b) deliver all in-progress Work Product to Company, (c) return or destroy Company materials, and (d) submit a final invoice for Services rendered through the termination date.

## 8. Warranties

Contractor warrants that:

8.1 Contractor has the right, authority, and capacity to enter into this Agreement;

8.2 The Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care consistent with industry standards;

8.3 The Work Product will be original to Contractor (except for incorporated Pre-Existing IP per the IP Assignment) and will not infringe any third-party rights;

8.4 Contractor is not bound by any conflicting obligation to a third party.

## 9. Indemnification

9.1 Contractor shall indemnify and hold Company harmless from any claim arising from (a) Contractor's breach of warranties in Section 8, (b) Contractor's breach of confidentiality, or (c) Contractor's negligent or willful misconduct in performing Services.

9.2 Company's indemnification of Contractor is limited to the total fees paid to Contractor under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## 10. Limitation of Liability

Except for breaches of confidentiality, IP Assignment, or Section 6, neither Party is liable to the other for consequential, incidental, indirect, or punitive damages, and aggregate liability is capped at the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement.

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 This Agreement, together with the SoW, NDA, and IP Assignment, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

11.2 No amendment is effective without written agreement signed by both Parties.

11.3 Governing law: Republic of Peru. Venue: Lima.

11.4 This Agreement may be executed electronically and in counterparts.

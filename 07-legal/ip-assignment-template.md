This IP Assignment Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into between:

**Orquor S.A.C.**, a Peruvian company ("Company")

and

**[Name of individual]** ("Assignor")

Effective Date: [DATE]

## 1. Background

Assignor is engaged by Company as [employee / contractor] to perform [role description] ("Services"). In connection with such Services, Assignor may conceive, develop, contribute to, or first reduce to practice inventions, works of authorship, source code, designs, processes, models, prompts, verifiers, evaluation frameworks, methodologies, datasets, brand assets, and other intellectual property (collectively, "Work Product").

## 2. Assignment

Assignor hereby irrevocably assigns to Company all right, title, and interest, worldwide, in and to any and all Work Product, including:

(a) all patent rights, including the right to file, prosecute, and maintain patent applications;
(b) all copyrights, including all moral rights to the extent assignable, and rights of paternity and integrity to the extent waivable;
(c) all trademark and trade-dress rights;
(d) all trade-secret rights and rights in confidential information;
(e) all rights in databases, datasets, model weights, and training corpora;
(f) all rights in software, source code, object code, scripts, and configurations;
(g) all rights in documentation, papers, whitepapers, blog posts, presentations, and creative works;
(h) any other intellectual property or proprietary right of any kind, in any jurisdiction.

This assignment applies whether or not the Work Product was created during working hours, on Company premises, or using Company resources, **so long as it is related to the Services or to the actual or contemplated business of Company**.

## 3. Pre-Existing IP

Assignor shall identify in **Exhibit A** any pre-existing intellectual property that Assignor wishes to exclude from Section 2 ("Pre-Existing IP"). If Assignor incorporates any Pre-Existing IP into Work Product, Assignor grants Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, modify, distribute, and sublicense such Pre-Existing IP solely in connection with the Work Product.

If Exhibit A is left blank, Assignor warrants there is no Pre-Existing IP relevant to the Services.

## 4. Further Assurances

Assignor agrees to execute any further documents, including specific patent or copyright assignments, that Company reasonably requests to perfect or record the assignment in Section 2. Assignor designates Company as Assignor's attorney-in-fact (with full power of substitution) for the limited purpose of executing such documents if Assignor is unavailable.

## 5. Disclosure Obligation

Assignor shall promptly disclose to Company in writing any Work Product or potential Work Product that Assignor conceives, develops, or first reduces to practice in connection with the Services.

## 6. Confidentiality

Assignor shall hold Company's Confidential Information (as defined in the separate NDA executed between the Parties or in Company's employee/contractor handbook) in strict confidence and shall not use or disclose Confidential Information except as authorized for the Services.

## 7. No Conflicting Obligations

Assignor represents and warrants that:

7.1 Assignor is not bound by any agreement, with any third party, that conflicts with this Agreement;

7.2 Assignor will not bring to Company or use in the course of Services any third-party intellectual property without written authorization;

7.3 The Work Product, except for incorporated Pre-Existing IP as listed in Exhibit A, will be original to Assignor.

## 8. Term and Survival

This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues through the term of engagement and indefinitely thereafter with respect to Work Product created during engagement. The assignment in Section 2 is perpetual and irrevocable.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Peru. Disputes shall be resolved exclusively in Lima courts.

# Orquor Sales — Prospect Master Sheet

Beachhead: clínicas de turismo médico en Costa Rica y Colombia. 10 organizaciones objetivo. Cultivación HUMINT-style — sin outbound spray. Ver `/orquor/canvases/orquor-capa-negra.canvas.tsx` Framework 1 para metodología completa.

**Status legend**
- `profiled` — asset profile completed, ready to engage
- `engaged` — ambient engagement ongoing (3+ public touchpoints)
- `signaled` — asset signaled interest (DM, reply, follow)
- `conversing` — active conversation
- `meeting` — meeting scheduled or held
- `pilot` — pilot agreement in motion
- `paying` — paying customer

## Cultivation discipline (the rules — NO EXCEPTIONS)

1. **No outbound spray.** Every contact is contextual. If we cannot find a hook, we wait.
2. **3-6 months ambient engagement before first DM.** Likes, comments with substance, shares of relevant content with caption.
3. **Whitepaper is the discovery vehicle.** Once ACTO whitepaper is published, that becomes the natural artifact to share with target asset.
4. **No pitch in first conversation.** Discovery and elicitation only. The question "what does this remind you of in your operation?" is more valuable than any product demo.
5. **One-touch per asset per week maximum** during ambient engagement phase. Over-touch is detected as spam.
6. **Document every interaction** in `/08-prospects/[CODE]-interactions.md` with date, channel, response/no-response, content of message.
7. **Calendar physical encounter at conferences.** Plan attendance at MTA Annual Conference, AHA-LatAm, eHealth LatAm.

## Monthly review

Last Friday of each month: review pipeline movement. Any asset that stayed in the same stage for 60 days without signal → downgrade to inactive, do not pursue until they signal first.

Any asset that escalated to `signaled` or further → +1 Friday review.

That's the Word Error Rate of Whisper Large v3 on medical terminology in English — the open baseline.

It's also why a hospital cannot just install OpenAI Whisper and call it medical translation. Eleven percent of medical words mis-transcribed is "the dose is ten point one milligrams" coming out as "the dose is one milligram". A decimal point lost. A life lost.

Our fine-tuned ASR for Orquor Clinical hits 4.1% on the ER specialty. Less than half the baseline. Still imperfect — which is exactly why every output goes through 60 verifiers before it reaches the screen.

Verifier-backed translation is not a feature. It's an architectural decision. You either ship outputs that are checked before delivery, or you ship outputs and hope.

The LATAM healthcare AI infrastructure funding landscape in 2026. A map for founders building clinical AI in the region.

This is not an exhaustive survey. It is the set of programs that a clinical AI company building in LATAM should have on its radar in 2026.

**Peru.**

- **Innóvate Perú** (Ministerio de la Producción). Competitive grants for technology ventures. The "Startup Perú" program (now in its 9th generation) provides up to PEN 150,000 (~USD 40,000) for early-stage ventures, up to PEN 500,000 (~USD 135,000) for scaling ventures. Eligibility: Peruvian-registered company, technology focus, validated prototype. Healthcare AI falls within scope.

- **ProCiencia** (CONCYTEC). Research and development grants for projects with academic partnership. Relevant if your clinical AI project has a university collaboration component. Typical grant size: PEN 200,000–800,000.

- **BID Lab** (via COFIDE and local partners). Innovation financing for early-stage ventures with measurable development impact. Healthcare access qualifies. Grant sizes vary; typical range USD 50,000–250,000.

**Colombia.**

- **iNNpulsa Colombia** (Ministerio de Comercio). The "Aldea" and "Crea" programs fund technology ventures at pre-seed and seed stages. Healthcare is a priority sector under the current administration.

- **Fondo de Ciencia, Tecnología e Innovación** (SGR). Royalty-funded grants for regional innovation projects. Requires regional government partnership. Relevant for clinical AI deployed in underserved regions.

- **Ruta N** (Medellín). City-level innovation agency with direct investment and soft-landing programs for healthtech ventures. One of the most founder-friendly entry points in LATAM.

**Mexico.**

- **INADEM** (Instituto Nacional del Emprendedor, currently under restructuring). Historically the largest startup funding vehicle in LATAM. The restructuring creates uncertainty but the mandate remains. Monitor for program announcements in H2 2026.

**Brazil.**

- **Finep** (Financiadora de Estudos e Projetos). Federal innovation financing. The "Finep Startup" program provides grants and equity investments for deep-tech ventures. Healthcare AI qualifies as deep-tech. Grant sizes: BRL 500,000–2,000,000 (~USD 100,000–400,000).

- **Sebrae** (Serviço Brasileiro de Apoio às Micro e Pequenas Empresas). Startup acceleration and market-access programs. Highly distributed, state-level offices. The "Sebrae Startups" program is the entry point.

**Multilateral.**

- **BID Lab** (across LATAM). Innovation financing with development impact criteria. Healthcare access and language-barrier reduction both qualify.
- **CAF** (Banco de Desarrollo de América Latina). Increasing activity in technology and innovation financing. The "CAF Digital" program is the relevant vehicle.
- **GSMA Innovation Fund.** Mobile-for-development grants. Relevant if your clinical AI has a mobile or telehealth component.

**The tactical note.** None of these programs will fund your entire round. The grants are supplementary — they extend runway, validate the venture for subsequent investors, and create government relationships that matter for regulated industries. Apply for the grant that requires the least administrative overhead first. Use the award to credential the company for the next, larger grant. The sequence compounds.

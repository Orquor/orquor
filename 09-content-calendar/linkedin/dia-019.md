To the certified medical interpreters reading this: the system is not designed to replace you. The economics do not work for replacement, and the clinical risk does not allow for it.

Here is what the actual deployment looks like.

A medium-sized hospital that handles 60 cross-language clinical encounters per day, today, pays a US VRI vendor approximately USD 130 per day per encounter in cumulative interpretation minutes. That is approximately USD 280,000 per year. The vendor pays the certified interpreter a fraction of that.

The same hospital with Orquor Clinical handles 45 of those encounters with the AI pipeline and 15 with a human interpreter routed by the orchestrator. The AI absorbs the structured high-frequency utterances — vitals, medication confirmation, basic anamnesis. The human takes the consequential moments — informed consent, family meetings, complex history of present illness, anything where the verifier suite flags uncertainty.

The economics for the interpreter are better, not worse. In the Orquor model, the interpreter handles 50 percent fewer total sessions but is paid 1.6 to 1.8 times the previous per-session rate. The reasons: each session is harder and more consequential, the interpreter is on retainer rather than per-call, and the platform takes a smaller commission because the AI carries the structured volume. The interpreter's hourly effective rate is 30 to 40 percent higher.

For the hospital: total cost drops by 35 to 45 percent. For the interpreter: pay per hour rises and the work is more interesting.

The interpreters who are nervous about AI in this space are nervous about the wrong system. The system designed to replace you is the system that ships an AI-only product, charges the hospital less than your current rate, and absorbs your entire volume. We have explicitly rejected that architecture. The verifier suite makes it impossible to deploy that way safely; the audit-log liability makes it untenable economically.

What we are building is the architecture in which the certified interpreter is the high-value resource at the top of an escalation stack — not the bottom. Your work becomes more skilled, not less. Your hourly compensation goes up, not down. Your job security comes from being the irreplaceable layer that handles the cases the system flags as too consequential for the AI alone.

If you are a certified medical interpreter in LATAM or the US-Hispanic market and you want to be part of this — orquor.com/careers/interpreters. We are hiring.

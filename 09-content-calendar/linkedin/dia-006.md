Lima is the right city to build this.

Six reasons.

One. The Spanish-medical vocabulary spoken in clinical Lima is the most regionally neutral dialect of the language. It maps cleanly to Bogotá, San José, Mexico City, Miami, Buenos Aires. A model trained on Lima clinical Spanish generalizes; a model trained on Buenos Aires or Mexico City does not.

Two. Peruvian software-engineering salaries are 30% of US Bay Area for senior talent. Our compute is the same NVIDIA H100. Our regulatory burden in this phase is one-tenth the US equivalent. The unit economics that look interesting in San Francisco look unmistakable in Lima.

Three. The Peruvian regulatory framework — Ley 29733 for personal data, SBS for financial services, MINSA for health, SUSALUD for private healthcare — is mature enough to be enforceable and flexible enough to be navigable. Brazil's LGPD is stricter. The US HIPAA stack is more crowded with players. Peru is the right launch jurisdiction for a regulated AI play in Spanish.

Four. The cluster of Lima clinical institutions — Clínica Anglo Americana, Clínica Internacional, San Pablo, AUNA — has a mature relationship with international patients and ergo a mature relationship with English. They speak the language of US insurance and US procurement. They are also experimenters: they will pilot something that the New York-based hospital network would put through six committees.

Five. The Spanish-speaking diaspora in the United States is 62 million people. Their healthcare is consistently underserved on the language axis. A company built in Lima that serves both LATAM and US-Hispanic healthcare has a market that no purely-US company can match on cultural fit, and no purely-LATAM company can match on regulatory rigor.

Six. We are not the only ones building this. We just happen to be doing it where the gravity of the right talent, the right language, and the right regulators is concentrated. The next decade of LATAM software is not going to be "outsourcing for the US". It is going to be "sovereign AI for the Spanish-speaking world". Orquor is the bet that this transition has a beginning, a middle, and a winner.

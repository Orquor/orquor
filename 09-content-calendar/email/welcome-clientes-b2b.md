Welcome to Orquor Clinical. The contract is signed, your tenant is provisioned, and I want to spend the next 90 seconds describing what happens in your first week.

**Today**

- Your tenant is live at clinical.orquor.com/[your-organization-slug]
- Your administrator account (your email) has been created with full provisioning rights
- The BAA is countersigned and on file; a copy is attached
- The custom verifier suite tuned for your specialty mix is loaded

**Day 1–3 — administrator setup**

Your administrator (typically the CIO or designated technical lead) completes:
- User provisioning for your clinical staff (SSO via your existing identity provider, or password-based with TOTP)
- Department / register configuration (which of the nine clinical specialties you enable, which registers are available per specialty)
- Audit log retention policy (default 10 years, configurable per regulator requirement)
- Integration with your scheduling system if you use one (Calendly, Outlook, or proprietary)

The documentation at clinical.orquor.com/docs covers each of these in 5 minutes per topic.

**Day 4–7 — first live sessions**

You will run your first 20–30 sessions in shadow mode. The AI runs end-to-end but the output is reviewed before being shown to the clinician. We sit on a Signal channel with your team for the first week. Latency to issue resolution is under 30 minutes during business hours and under 2 hours overnight.

By the end of week 1, your team has seen what the verifier results look like, how the audit log is exposed in the dashboard, and how to invoke the on-demand human interpreter pool.

**Week 2 onward**

You enter normal production operation. We send a weekly performance digest:
- Sessions handled, latency p50/p95/p99
- Verifier pass rates by specialty
- Escalation rates to human interpreter
- Any audit log irregularities (there should be none)

You also receive automated alerts if any threshold drifts outside the SLA we agreed to.

**Important — audit log access**

You own your audit log. You can download it at any time, in any time range, in JSON or CSV format, with the cryptographic timestamps included. You can hand the file to any independent auditor and they can verify it against the underlying OpenTimestamps anchors without involving us.

This is what we mean by defensibility. Your evidence is your own, mathematically.

Reply directly to this email and I personally read it within 24 hours. For urgent technical issues, support@orquor.com routes to our on-call rotation with a 30-minute response SLA during business hours.

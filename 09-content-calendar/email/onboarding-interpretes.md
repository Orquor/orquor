Hola [First Name],

Welcome to Orquor. You are joining a small team of certified medical interpreters working at the intersection of clinical accuracy and AI orchestration. I want to spend the next 90 seconds telling you what to expect in your first 30 days.

**Days 1–3 — orientation and access**

You will receive separate emails with:
- Your Orquor email account ([firstname]@orquor.com)
- Access to the interpreter console at console.orquor.com
- Calendar integration for shift scheduling
- Signal handle for the on-call channel
- The BAA cover letter (you signed the BAA at offer; this is just for your records)

You will be paired with [Mentor Name], a senior interpreter who handled your final certification verification. Schedule a 30-minute kickoff call in the first week.

**Days 4–10 — observation**

You will observe live sessions in a read-only mode. You will see what the AI does, where the orchestrator routes uncertainty to a human, what the verifier results look like, and how the per-utterance audit log is constructed. No pressure to act. Just absorb the system.

By the end of day 10, you should be able to answer: "what does the orchestrator do when a verifier fails?" and "what is the cryptographic anchor in the audit log?". If either is unclear, your mentor walks you through it.

**Days 11–20 — supervised practice**

You begin handling escalated utterances with your mentor monitoring. Every translation you produce is logged and reviewed inside 24 hours. We track the agreement rate between your translation and the AI-suggested translation, and the cases where you diverged and why. The divergence cases are the most valuable training data we have.

**Days 21–30 — full production**

You go live, full session ownership, no mentor monitoring. You become a primary node in the routing graph. Your hourly rate adjusts to the full production rate documented in your offer.

**A few things I want to be explicit about**

The system is designed to make your work more skilled and better-paid, not less. You will handle fewer total minutes than you did at LanguageLine or a comparable vendor — but your per-session pay rate is materially higher, the cases you take are the consequential ones, and your professional standing is documented in the audit log of every session you touch. Read the whitepaper on Auditable Clinical Translation Orchestration if you have not already: orquor.com/whitepaper-acto.pdf. Section 4 explains the role you play in the architecture.

If you have questions or hit anything frustrating, reply directly. I read every interpreter email within 24 hours.

Welcome aboard.

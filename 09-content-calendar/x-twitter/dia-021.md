**1/**
30 days of Orquor operating in public. The short version of what I learned.

**2/**
What worked:

→ Technical depth > polish. Every post with a Python function or benchmark number outperformed vision statements by 4–6× engagement.
→ Open whitepaper was the highest-leverage decision. Inbound from people who would not respond to outbound exceeded pre-launch pipeline.
→ Interpreter-direct positioning resonated. Certified interpreters replied because we talked to them, not past them.

**3/**
What did not work:

→ LinkedIn carousels. 40% lower engagement vs text. This audience wants paragraphs.
→ Generic "AI is changing healthcare" framings. Immediate scroll-past.
→ Broad-awareness spend. ROI invisible at this stage. Budget pulled back into product.

**4/**
What surprised me:

→ ACTO vocabulary propagated in 9 days, not the 60 I budgeted.
→ First inbound was from regulators, not hospital CIOs.
→ Two competitors started using "audit log" in their marketing within a week.

The vocabulary is spreading before the product is.

**5/**
What I changed my mind about:

→ Procurement signal flows from regulator attention to CIO attention, not the reverse.
→ Second product to ship is more likely Orquor Telehealth API than Orquor Triage.
→ Production polish does not move the needle. Substance does.

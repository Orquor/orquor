Practical learnings:

→ Putting a Python function on screen consistently outperformed putting a vision statement on screen by 4-6× engagement
→ Posts that named specific competitors (with intent and respect) outperformed neutral framing
→ Long-form (12-min YouTube) drove higher-quality leads than short-form

The lesson is uniform: technical specificity wins over polish.

What didn't work:

→ LinkedIn carousels in the first week (low engagement vs text)
→ Generic "AI is changing healthcare" framings (immediate scroll-past)
→ Trying to compete on visual production quality with established creators

Build authority through depth, not gloss.

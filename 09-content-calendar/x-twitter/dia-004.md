**1/**
"Clinical-grade" is the most abused phrase in healthcare AI marketing in 2026.

Here is what it actually means in technical terms. If a vendor cannot produce all six, the phrase is decoration.

**2/**
1 — Specialty-tuned model.

Generic Whisper is not clinical-grade ASR. Generic GPT-4 is not clinical-grade MT. The model must be fine-tuned on a medical corpus that includes the actual specialty vocabulary in production. If the vendor cannot show the dataset description, the tuning did not happen.

**3/**
2 — Verifier suite, not just a confidence score.

"Confidence 0.92" is a model self-report. A verifier is an external deterministic test. Clinical-grade means at least one verifier per known failure mode, with weighted gating. Confidence alone is consumer-grade.

**4/**
3 — Per-utterance audit log.

Session-level metadata is consumer-grade. Per-utterance log with structured pass/fail and timestamp is clinical-grade. The granularity difference is what survives an adverse-event investigation.

**5/**
4 — Cryptographic anchoring.

The audit log must be tamper-evident. RFC 3161 timestamping authority or OpenTimestamps anchored to Bitcoin. Without this, the log is a database record — modifiable, deniable.

**6/**
5 — Confidential computing for PHI.
6 — BAA + signed compliance posture (HIPAA, GDPR, LGPD, Law 29733).

Six criteria. All six required to deserve the phrase.

If your vendor checks 3 of 6, they are healthcare-flavored. Not clinical-grade.

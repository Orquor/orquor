**1/**
Your clinical AI product's audit log is either a forensic primitive or a compliance checkbox.

The difference determines whether a translation error becomes a legal liability or a defended clinical decision.

Most vendors shipped the checkbox. Here is what the primitive looks like.

**2/**
Write-before-act.

The orchestrator writes the routing decision to the log *before* the translation reaches the screen. Not after.

If verifiers pass: "verified, AI route, suite v1.7, all passed" → then deliver.
If verifiers fail: "escalated to human, reason V2 dosage mismatch" → then block delivery.

The log is causal, not retrospective.

**3/**
Cryptographic timestamping.

Each log entry hash → OpenTimestamps calendar → Merkle tree → Bitcoin blockchain.

Result: a .ots proof file that any regulator or court can independently verify. The timestamps cannot be backdated. The log cannot be tampered with after the fact.

Cost: ~USD 10/day for a hospital deployment. One litigation episode where log integrity is challenged: orders of magnitude more.

**4/**
The legal posture shift:

Without write-before-act + timestamps: plaintiff's expert asks "was this logged before or after delivery?" Vendor cannot answer. Posture weakened.

With: log proves the error was intercepted at 14:32:07.441 and corrected at 14:32:09.882. Timestamp is cryptographically verifiable. Posture strengthened conclusively.

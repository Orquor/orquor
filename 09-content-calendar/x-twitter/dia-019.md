The economics of cooperative AI in clinical translation, in five numbers.

Most people assume AI + human = more expensive. The math says the opposite.

**2/**
45 of 60 daily encounters: AI handles structured high-frequency utterances — vitals, medication, basic anamnesis.

15 of 60: human interpreter handles consequential moments — consent, family meetings, complex history.

The interpreter handles 50% fewer sessions. The sessions are harder and better compensated.

**3/**
Hospital total cost: drops 35–45% vs current VRI model.

Interpreter pay per session: rises 1.6–1.8×.

Interpreter effective hourly rate: rises 30–40%.

The AI absorbs the structured volume. The human absorbs the consequential volume. Both economics improve.

**4/**
The comparison that matters:

→ AI-only: charges less than market, absorbs full volume, interpreter replaced, liability unmanaged.
→ Cooperative inference: charges below AI-only + human combined, interpreter retained at higher hourly, liability managed via verifier suite + audit log.

The cooperative model wins on economics, safety, and interpreter retention.

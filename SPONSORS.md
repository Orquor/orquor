# Sponsors

Orquor builds open infrastructure for auditable clinical translation. The public tooling — Hermes, verifier suites, ACTO specification — is maintained without direct commercial return. Sponsors keep it alive.

## Why sponsor

- **You operate in healthcare.** Contributions directly fund translation tooling that clinics in LATAM depend on today.
- **You build with AI agents.** Hermes is an open multi-agent orchestration platform. Sponsorship accelerates the public roadmap.
- **You want the ACTO category to exist.** Sponsors make the open specification defensible without patents.

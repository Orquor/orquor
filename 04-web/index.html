What we build

A new category for AI in regulated work. The current market splits into expensive human-only services and fast-but-unaccountable AI translation. Orquor occupies the third position.

01

Verifier-backed inference

Every output from an Orquor pipeline passes through a weighted outcome-based test suite before it reaches the user. Failures are surfaced, not silenced.

02

Cooperative agents

AI components and human specialists operate as a single orchestrated pipeline. The right resource handles the right utterance, every time.

03

Court-grade audit logs

Every session is recorded with per-utterance cryptographic timestamping (RFC 3161 / OpenTimestamps). The log is portable and externally verifiable.

A new category

Auditable Clinical Translation Orchestration

We call this ACTO. It is the category that resolves the quality-cost-defensibility trade-off that bifurcates medical interpretation in 2026. We are publishing the technical specification openly so the industry can rise together.

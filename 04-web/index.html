Category design

What we build

A third category for AI in regulated work. The market splits into expensive human-only services and fast-but-unaccountable AI translation. Orquor occupies the position in between: fast enough for clinical workflows, rigorous enough for regulatory scrutiny.

01

Verifier-backed inference

Every output from an Orquor pipeline passes through a weighted, outcome-based test suite before reaching the user. Failures are surfaced, not silenced. The system ships with a verifier coverage report for every deployment.

02

Cooperative orchestration

AI agents and human specialists operate as a single pipeline. The right resource handles the right utterance. Handoffs are protocol-driven and logged end-to-end.

03

Court-grade audit logs

Every session is recorded with per-utterance cryptographic timestamping (RFC 3161 / OpenTimestamps). The log is portable, tamper-evident, and externally verifiable.
